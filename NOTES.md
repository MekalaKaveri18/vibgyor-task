# Notes

**Identifying plans and walls.** The DXF is one sheet (metres). I located each drawing by its title text (`1ST TO 7TH FLOOR PLAN`, `SECTION - 'A-A'`, ...) and fixed it as a box in `config.toml`; the extractor stops if a title is not inside its box. The tower footprint is the closed `A_BUILT UP` polyline of 13.12 × 38.73 m (the printed dimensions) and is the model's origin. Walls are the `g-wall` / `g-r.c.c` lines: each wall is drawn as two faces, so a morphological closing (r = 0.13 m) fuses them into one solid while rooms and gaps stay open. Openings are the `g-dw` frames, named by the nearest `W1`/`SD`... label and sized from the printed *schedule of openings*. Floor-to-floor 3.05 m comes from the section's slab labels, the cabin and lift-room tops from its roof slabs. The site is drawn four times; plot, roads and the 25 trees come from the landscape copy, registered to the tower via the plot outline. Type A/B come from the plan text (`101 TO 701 … 160.05` is the flat nearer the 18 m road).

**Config.** Only what a 2D plan cannot say: DXF region per drawing, this CAD template's layer names, constants read off annotations (0.6 m cantilever, 1.15 m guard height, 2.10 m door head, 0.15 m slab), material choices, render settings. Values that are *my assumptions* are marked as such in the file: wall thickness at openings, the entrance canopy (not drawn), planter height, gate position.

**What breaks / assumptions.** Layer names and the one-sheet layout are specific to this drawing. Floors 1-7 are one extruded typical plan; walls thicker than ~0.26 m would not fuse. Not modelled: basement and ramp, stair flights, the brochure's curved fascia. Louvres, planters, hedges, parked cars and the 14 scenery trees are rule-based or seeded, not drawn. Elevation and section disagree on cabin height (26.3 vs 27.9 m); I used the section. Cycles' refractive glass rendered black on thin panes, so glazing is a Fresnel mix. Windows facing away from the sun look dark at dusk. The panorama furniture is simple boxes placed from the room labels. Renders are CPU-only (about 1.5 h for the four stills, ~2 h more for the panorama). Only tested on Windows.

**Hours.** ~45 h (brief: 25-30 h; the extra went on the panorama bonus and rendering iterations).
